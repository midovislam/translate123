Privacy Policy

translate123 — translate123.app

Effective Date: March 22, 2026

Short summary: translate123 does not store your audio, photos, or text on any server. All data is processed in real-time through third-party AI services and immediately discarded.

1. Who We Are

translate123 is an AI-powered translation application available at translate123.app. If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at midov.islam.m@gmail.com.

2. Data We Collect and How We Use It

We collect only what is necessary to provide the translation service:

  • Audio recordings — captured via your device microphone when you use voice translation. Audio is sent directly to OpenAI's Whisper API for real-time transcription and is not stored on our servers.
  • Photos — captured via your device camera or uploaded from your library when you use photo translation. Images are sent directly to OpenAI's GPT-4o-mini API for OCR (text extraction) and are not stored on our servers.
  • Text input — text you type or paste is sent to OpenAI's GPT-4o-mini API for translation and is not stored on our servers.
  • Usage tracking — anonymous usage counts (e.g., credit consumption) are tracked via Upstash Redis solely to manage the free-credit system. No personal identifiers are linked to these counts.
  • Analytics — we use Microsoft Clarity and Vercel Analytics to understand general usage patterns (page views, clicks). These tools may collect anonymised session data such as browser type and interaction events. No personally identifiable information is collected by these tools.

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not create or store user accounts.
  • We do not store audio, photos, or translated text on our servers.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade any data to third parties.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or profiling purposes.

4. API Keys and Local Storage

translate123 uses a Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) model. When you provide your OpenAI API key, it is stored only in your browser's localStorage — it is never transmitted to or stored on our servers. You are responsible for the security of your own API key.

5. Free Credits

New users receive $1 in free translation credits funded through the creator's API key. Usage against these free credits is tracked anonymously via Upstash Redis to prevent abuse. Once free credits are exhausted, you are prompted to provide your own OpenAI API key.

6. Device Permissions

  • Microphone — required for voice translation. The app requests microphone access only when you initiate a voice translation. You may deny this permission; voice translation will be unavailable.
  • Camera — required for photo translation. The app requests camera access only when you choose to capture a photo for translation. You may deny this permission and upload a photo from your library instead.

7. Third-Party Services

translate123 relies on the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • OpenAI — handles all AI processing (transcription, OCR, translation). See OpenAI Privacy Policy.
  • Vercel — hosts the application. See Vercel Privacy Policy.
  • Upstash Redis — anonymous usage/credit tracking. See Upstash Privacy Policy.
  • Microsoft Clarity — session analytics. See Microsoft Privacy Statement.
  • Vercel Analytics — page-view analytics. See Vercel Privacy Policy.

8. Children's Privacy

translate123 is not designed for or directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used our service, please contact us at midov.islam.m@gmail.com and we will take appropriate action.

9. Data Retention

Because we do not store your audio, photos, or text, there is no user content to retain or delete. Anonymous usage counters in Upstash Redis are retained only as long as needed to manage the free-credit system.

10. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights regarding your personal data (e.g., access, correction, deletion). Since we hold no personally identifiable data, most requests can be satisfied by simply clearing your browser's localStorage. For any questions or requests, contact us at midov.islam.m@gmail.com.

11. Security

We use HTTPS for all data transmission. Because data is processed in real-time and not stored, the attack surface is minimal. Your API key is stored only in your browser; keep your device secure.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at translate123.app/privacy-policy with an updated effective date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
